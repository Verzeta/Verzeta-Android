# Browse conversations

The **Chat** tab opens the Conversations screen, where you browse every chat on the paired desktop. Chats are grouped into five sections that match the desktop sidebar.

## Sections

- **Pinned**: conversations you have pinned.
- **Projects & Organizations**: project and organization folders. Tap a folder to expand it. You see its team members, a **Start Group Chat with All** row when the folder has two or more members, and its group chats and direct chats. Regular folders are listed here too while **All folders** is on in the Folders screen.
- **Standalone Group Chats**: group chats that are not in a project or organization.
- **Direct Agent Chats**: 1:1 chats with a specific agent that are not in a folder.
- **Plain Chats**: chats with no primary agent and no folder.

Tap a section header to collapse or expand it. The app remembers which sections are collapsed until you close it.

## Search

The search bar above the list filters conversations by title. Search runs on your phone. Your search text is never sent to the desktop.

## Open a conversation

Tap a conversation to open it. On a large tablet or a DeX or Chromebook window in landscape, and on the unfolded inner screen of a foldable in either orientation, the list and the chat appear side by side, so you can browse and chat at the same time.

## Conversation actions

Long-press a conversation to see these actions:

- **Rename**: change the title.
- **Pin** or **Unpin**: move it into or out of the Pinned section.
- **Move to folder**: move it into a project or organization, or back to the root.
- **Delete**: remove it permanently, after you confirm.

## Folder actions

Long-press a folder header for **Edit folder** and **Delete folder**. **Edit folder** opens the folder's settings sheet; see "Change a folder's settings" on the Projects and rooms page.

Deleting a folder does not delete the conversations inside it. They move to the root.

## Open a 1:1 chat with a team member

When a project or organization folder is expanded, each team member appears as a row with their alias, for example `@coordinator`. Tap a member to open your 1:1 chat with that agent. If one does not exist yet, the app creates it.

## Start a group chat with all members

In an expanded folder with two or more members, tap **Start Group Chat with All**. The New Group screen opens with that folder's members already added.

## Create a conversation, group chat or folder

Tap **New** at the bottom of the screen to open the create menu:

- **New chat**: a 1:1 conversation. If the desktop has a model selected, the chat opens at once. Otherwise the model picker opens first.
- **New group chat**: opens the New Group screen, where you name the chat and pick its agent members.
- **New folder / project**: opens the Folders screen with the New folder sheet already open.

## What this screen does not do

- **Search across desktops.** Search only matches conversations on the desktop you are connected to.
- **Change several conversations at once.** You change one conversation at a time. There is no multi-select.
- **Export.** The app cannot export conversations. Use the desktop instead.
