# Polls

Polls are voting cards that appear in the chat. An agent creates one with its `start_poll` tool, usually when you ask it to. Polls are most useful in group chats and project rooms, where they settle a choice without pulling the whole conversation off topic.

## What a poll looks like

A poll appears inside the agent's message and shows:

- The question
- Who created it, the vote mode, and the number of votes and voters
- Each option with its share of the votes
- An **OPEN** or **CLOSED** badge
- The time left, if the poll has a closing time

Tap an option to vote. The counts update live as other paired devices vote.

## Start a poll

Ask an agent, for example: "Start a poll on what we should call this feature."

## Vote modes

- **Single**: pick exactly one option.
- **Multi**: pick any number of options.

Ranked-choice polls are not supported.

## Closing a poll

A poll can close by itself after a set time, chosen when it is created, or anyone in the chat can tap **Close poll**.

When a poll closes:

- It accepts no more votes.
- The card shows the result: "Winner: ...", "Closed with a tie." or "Closed with no votes."
- The card keeps its final state in the chat history.

## Polls and history

A poll stays attached to its message, so scrolling back shows the final result.

## What polls do not do

- **Anonymous voting.** Voters' aliases are recorded.
- **Voting by link.** Only devices paired with the desktop can vote.
- **Quorums or thresholds.** The option with the most votes wins.
